# TAQWA VANCE Website – Recommended Starting Phase

## Final Recommendation
**Start with Phase 2: Semi-Portal Website (forms + basic dashboards).**

This gives TAQWA VANCE a professional system feel from day one without the cost/risk of a full custom platform.

## Why Phase 2 is Best Right Now
1. **Matches your vision** (Education + Business + Welfare + Portal entry points).
2. **Launches fast** compared to building a full custom app first.
3. **Builds trust early** through Request Help forms, transparent workflows, and impact counters.
4. **Collects real user data** (students, volunteers, clients, welfare cases) before advanced automation.
5. **Creates clean upgrade path** to Phase 3 when traction and funding increase.

## What to Build in Phase 2
- Home page with clear 3-step model and division overview.
- Dedicated pages:
  - Education & Career
  - Technology & Innovation
  - Business & Entrepreneurship
  - Welfare & Help Platform
  - Join Us
- Working forms:
  - Join as Student / Volunteer / Partner / Client
  - Request Help
  - Sponsor a Case
- Basic impact dashboard counters.
- WhatsApp quick contact + mobile-first UI.

## 60-Day Delivery Roadmap
### Weeks 1–2
- Brand style setup (dark blue, green, white).
- Core page wireframes and content structure.

### Weeks 3–4
- Build website pages and navigation.
- Connect all forms to response tracking sheets/CRM.

### Weeks 5–6
- Add impact dashboard + transparency section.
- QA for mobile devices and performance optimization.

### Weeks 7–8
- Launch campaign.
- Measure conversion: joins, help requests, client leads.
- Prepare technical scope document for Phase 3 portal.

## Success Metrics for Phase 2
- 200+ student signups
- 50+ volunteer registrations
- 20+ business leads
- Verified welfare request handling pipeline live

## Decision Statement
**TAQWA VANCE should begin with Phase 2 now, then move to Phase 3 after validated demand and operational data.**
